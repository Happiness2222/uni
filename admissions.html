Undergraduate Admission

Admission is open for qualified candidates into all accredited undergraduate programmes.

Admission Requirements

  • Minimum of 3 O'Level Credits.
  • Valid UTME Result.
  • Post-UTME Screening.
  • Birth Certificate.
  • State of Origin Certificate.

🗓 Admission Process

  1. Create an account.
  2. Complete the admission form.
  3. Upload required documents.
  4. Pay the admission fee.
  5. Submit your admission form.
